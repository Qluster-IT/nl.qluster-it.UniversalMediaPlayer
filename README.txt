Browse and play music from a UPnP server. Choose direct playback on a speaker or group, or keep playback controlled by Homey Flows.

Play directly on Sonos, Google Cast and compatible UPnP/DLNA speakers or saved speaker groups. Control playback, browse the library and choose a speaker from the Media player dashboard widget.

Select a file or folder in a Flow card, then connect the Playback URL changed trigger to your speaker's play-URL action. The app looks up the current URL using the saved path. For Flow-controlled playback, connect Playback stopped to your speaker’s pause or stop action.
